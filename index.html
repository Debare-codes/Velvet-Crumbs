01 — The Cocoa Bean

Single-Origin Cacao, Roasted & Conched In-House

We partner directly with heirloom cacao growers in Ecuador and Madagascar. Every batch is lightly roasted to preserve natural notes of roasted hazelnut, dark cherry, and warm espresso—never masked by excess sugar.

Hand-Tempered

Crystallized on chilled marble slabs for an unmistakable crisp snap.

Zero Palm Oils

Pure cocoa butter and cultured Normandy-style cream only.

02 — The Pasture Cream

Slow-Churned Cultured Butter & Sweet Jersey Milk

Great chocolate deserves an equally expressive dairy counterpart. Our milk is sourced from grass-fed Jersey herds within 45 miles of our ovens, cultured for 24 hours for tangy depth, and whipped into cloud-like Chantilly and caramelized milk jams.
